The x-axis counts new acquisition proposals per task, excluding the common initial observations (49 query plans or 100 peptides). SemanticOPT values are carried forward after its completed budgets (200 query and 1,000 peptide proposals); completed baseline tasks similarly retain their final measured incumbents when either budget is exhausted. Peptide recentering evaluations incur additional oracle calls and do not increment the acquisition-proposal axis. FSBO uses the frozen-VAE local/recenter variant; TPE uses the global independent latent-space variant. SemanticOPT includes constrained random fallback proposals (81.7% query, 68.1% peptide).
